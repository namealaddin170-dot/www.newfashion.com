Élodie Haven fondatrice & directrice

After a decade at Maison Margiela and Balenciaga, Élodie opened her own atelier in Le Marais. Her work explores the tension between structure and fluidity — each piece is constructed around the movement of the body, using heritage textiles and unexpected draping.

“I design for those who see clothing as armor and poetry at once.”

— e.h.
